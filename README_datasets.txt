
Here are some selected datasets to test magnetic field measurements

There is three folder corresponding to different observations of the 2025 campaign

* 250206_AR13981flaring: Observation with good seeing conditions
    I have put the first sequence observed, t001_... in Fe I at 6300 (camera 303) and halpha (camera 606)
    I have included quicklook images of the Ha obs
    The folder contains aslo the corresponding flat field "t013_..." and a dark "t058_..."


* 250523_CHjet: Observation of a coronal hole jet, during Jade's campaign
    I have put a sequence observed in Fe I at 6300 (camera 303) and halpha (camera 606): "t039..."
    I have included quicklook images of the Ha obs
    The folder contains aslo the corresponding flat field "t027_..." but there is no dark

#Multi-ray spectrograph MTR2
# MTR2 is made of a predispersor (“SP1” first one on the left part), 
# forming a low resolution spectrum on the wavelength selector 
# (so called the “mask plate”). From there, only the selected bandwiths (masks)
# travel to the high-resolution echelle spectrograph (right part), 
# ending over the MTR cameras at the SP2 output (one bandwith per camera).
